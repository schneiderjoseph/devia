# RWD-001 — Touch targets adequate

**Requirement:** Primary touch targets MUST be comfortably tappable; meet A11Y-010 at minimum.

**Bad:** Dense icon row unusable on phone.

**Good:** 44×44 primary actions; spacing between targets.
