# OBS-002 — Errors reach a system a human watches

**Requirement:** Unhandled errors and failed background work MUST be reported to an error tracker or alert channel with enough context to reproduce, and MUST NOT be swallowed by an empty catch.

**Bad:** `catch (e) {}` and a job that fails silently every night.

**Good:** The error is reported with the correlation id and the job name; the failure is visible without asking.

## Validation

- No empty catch in the diff
- The new failure path reports somewhere
