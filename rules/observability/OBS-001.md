# OBS-001 — Logs are structured, correlated, and free of secrets

**Requirement:** Logs MUST be structured, MUST carry a request or trace identifier, and MUST NOT contain credentials, tokens, full payment data or unnecessary personal data.

**Bad:** `console.log(request.body)` on a payment route.

**Good:** A structured entry with the correlation id, the operation, the outcome and safe identifiers.

## Validation

- Grep the diff for raw body or header logging
- Correlation id present on the new paths

- Status: `active`
- Priority: `P1`
- Exceptions: `documented-only`
