# CNT-001 — Action-oriented labels

**Requirement:** Buttons SHOULD say the outcome (‘Save changes’), not vague OK/Submit when context is unclear.

**Bad:** Modal: OK / Cancel with ambiguous title.

**Good:** Delete customer / Keep customer.

- Design or engineering review
