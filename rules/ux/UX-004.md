# UX-004 — Depth with orientation

**Requirement:** Hierarchies deeper than two levels SHOULD provide orientation (breadcrumbs, clear titles, back).

**Bad:** Deep page with only a logo home link.

**Good:** Title + breadcrumb or explicit back to parent context.

- Design or engineering review
