# UX-006 — Skip to content

**Requirement:** Sites/apps with repeated chrome MUST provide a skip link or equivalent landmark bypass.

**Bad:** 100 tabs through the mega-nav every page load.

**Good:** Skip link or landmark structure that works with SR/keyboard.

## Validation

- Automated checks / lint / a11y tooling where applicable
- Design or engineering review
