# UX-011 — Confirm irreversible actions

**Requirement:** Destructive/irreversible actions MUST require confirmation or undo.

**Bad:** Delete permanently on single click.

**Good:** Confirm dialog with consequence stated, or toast undo window.

## Validation

- Design or engineering review
