# SEC-008 — Abuse surfaces are rate limited

**Requirement:** Authentication, password reset, invitation, search, export and any expensive or enumerable endpoint MUST be rate limited per actor and per source, and MUST fail closed when the limiter is unavailable.

**Bad:** Unlimited login attempts, or a limiter that silently allows everything when its backend is down.

**Good:** Per-account and per-IP limits with a clear 429, and a closed default when the limiter cannot answer.

## Validation

- Test: the limit triggers
- Confirm behaviour when the limiter backend fails
