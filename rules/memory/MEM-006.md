# MEM-006 — Sweep the surface you touch

**Requirement:** A change to a shared surface — schema, route family, port, interface, design token — MUST sweep that surface for other violations and record what it finds. Finding nothing is a valid result and MUST be stated.

**Bad:** Fixing one endpoint's authorization and leaving the four siblings unexamined and unrecorded.

**Good:** All five are checked; three are fine; two become debt lines.

## Validation

- The report states the swept surface and the outcome
