# CMP-001 — Button hierarchy

**Requirement:** A view MUST NOT present multiple primary buttons of equal weight for competing actions.

**Bad:** Three filled primary buttons in one toolbar.

**Good:** One primary; others secondary/tertiary/ghost.

## Validation

- Design or engineering review
