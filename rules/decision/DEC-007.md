# DEC-007 — Product intent is decided before the surface that carries it

**Requirement:** Purpose, audience and the primary action a visitor should take MUST be recorded before a surface whose job is to carry them is built. A surface built without them MUST NOT be reported as done.

**Bad:** A finished landing page on which nobody can say what the product does, or what the visitor is meant to do next.

**Good:** `product.purpose`, `product.audience` and `product.primary_action` are decided, and the page is reviewed against them.

## Validation

- The product slots are decided or delegated
- Review names the primary action the surface implements
