# DB-005 — Destructive operations are recoverable where the domain requires it

**Requirement:** Records that carry history, money or accountability SHOULD be archived or soft-deleted rather than hard-deleted, and the choice SHOULD be recorded in the data model.

**Bad:** `DELETE FROM users` on account closure, taking the audit trail with it.

**Good:** `archived_at` plus a retention job that removes personal data on schedule.

## Validation

- The data model states the deletion strategy per entity
