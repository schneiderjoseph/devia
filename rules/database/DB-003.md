# DB-003 — Money is exact and carries its currency

**Requirement:** Monetary amounts MUST use an exact type (decimal or minor-unit integer), never a float, and MUST be stored with an explicit currency. Rounding MUST happen at a single documented point.

**Bad:** `price REAL` and a currency inferred from the user's locale at render time.

**Good:** `amount numeric(14,2)` plus `currency char(3)`, rounded once in the pricing service.

## Validation

- Grep the migration for float or double on money columns
- Confirm currency is stored, not inferred
